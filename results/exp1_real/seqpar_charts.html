Sequence vs Parallel

Two ways of asking the same four forecasting harnesses the same questions on the same evidence. Sequence: 5 rollouts that walk the five dates in order, carrying their own forecasts and memory. Parallel: for each date, 5 fresh one-shot runs given everything up to that date and no history at all — the evidence-conditioned belief as a distribution. Every figure below is one lens on the difference; each has a hover layer and a data table.

Data: hockey pair (USA resolves YES, Canada NO; both reached the final inside the window, decided after it). Same model, same date-gated corpus, same tools in both conditions. gpt-5-mini; per-date parallel n = 5; sequential rollouts k = 5.

F3 · Anchoring summary

Right: lag advantage = |gap to today's fresh belief| − |gap to yesterday's|. Positive = the sequence tracks yesterday's evidence better than today's — it updates one date behind.
